Soil Health Guidelines:
1. Loamy soil is considered the best for most crops as it retains moisture well while allowing excess water to drain.
2. The optimal pH for tomato plants is between 6.0 and 6.8. If the soil is too acidic, add agricultural lime.
3. Adding organic compost increases soil fertility and microbial activity, which naturally suppresses soil-borne diseases.
4. Red soil is rich in iron oxide but poor in nitrogen, phosphorus, and humus. It requires regular fertilization to support crops like groundnuts and potatoes.
