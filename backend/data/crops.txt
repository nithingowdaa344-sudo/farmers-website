Crop Specific Information:
1. Maize (Corn) requires warm weather and plenty of water. It thrives best in well-drained loamy soils.
2. Paddy (Rice) is heavily water-dependent. It should be grown in fields where standing water can be maintained for long periods. Clayey soils are best for paddy.
3. Cotton needs a dry climate during the flowering and boll-opening stages. Black soil is highly recommended for cotton cultivation due to its high moisture-retaining capacity.
