Karnataka Agriculture Master Dataset

Karnataka has 10 agro-climatic zones based on:
- rainfall
- soil type
- humidity
- elevation
- crop pattern
- vegetation

Average annual rainfall:
Coastal Karnataka: up to 3000 mm
North dry region: around 600 mm

1. Soil Types in Karnataka
Soil Type | Regions | Characteristics | Suitable Crops
Black Soil | North Karnataka | High clay, retains moisture | Cotton, jowar, sunflower
Red Soil | South Karnataka | Iron-rich, less fertile | Ragi, groundnut, pulses
Laterite Soil | Western Ghats | Acidic, high rainfall zones | Coffee, tea, rubber
Alluvial Soil | River belts | Fertile, sandy | Sugarcane, paddy
Sandy Soil | Coastal Karnataka | Drains quickly | Coconut, cashew
Loamy Soil | Mixed regions | Balanced nutrients | Vegetables, fruits

Common soil pH:
Acidic: 5.5–6.5
Neutral: 6.5–7.5
Alkaline: 7.5–8.5

7. Leaf Disease Detection Data
Nitrogen Deficiency Symptoms: Yellow older leaves, Slow growth
Potassium Deficiency Symptoms: Brown leaf edges, Weak stems
Phosphorus Deficiency Symptoms: Purple leaves, Stunted roots
Fungal Infection Symptoms: White powder, Black spots
Bacterial Disease Symptoms: Water-soaked spots, Leaf curling
Viral Disease Symptoms: Mosaic patterns, Yellow streaks

11. Smart Bot Decision Rules
- IF soil is black AND humidity is low: Recommend Cotton.
- IF leaf color is yellow: Recommend Nitrogen Fertilizer.
- IF rainfall is less than 600mm: Recommend Millets.

12. Important Karnataka Agriculture Facts
- Karnataka agriculture heavily depends on southwest monsoon.
- Karnataka has 10 agro-climatic zones.
- Rainfall varies from 593 mm to 3085 mm.
- Climate change is altering crop patterns.
- Pulses and millets are increasing in cultivation.
